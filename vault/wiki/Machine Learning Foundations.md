# Machine Learning Foundations

**Summary:** This class introduces the fundamental concepts of machine learning, focusing on how models learn from data and make predictions. It emphasizes the increasing importance of AI/ML fluency for professionals across various industries, particularly in business roles requiring technical understanding of AI proposals and model building. The class also explores why AI is often preferred over traditional coding approaches, particularly when dealing with complex problems like chess.

**Key Concepts:**

*   **Model Learning:** Machine learning models learn patterns from data and use these patterns to predict outcomes for new data.
*   **Training:** The process of adjusting a model’s parameters based on training data to improve its predictive accuracy.
*   **Overfitting:** A situation where a model learns the training data too well, leading to poor performance on new, unseen data.
*   **Industry Demand:** There’s rising demand for professionals with AI/ML fluency in industries like finance, consulting, and healthcare.
*   **Evaluating AI Proposals:** Understanding a model’s data needs, performance measurements, and potential errors is crucial for evaluating AI proposals.
*   **Scaling AI:** Larger models, more data, and increased computing power are driving advancements in AI, enabling more complex and accurate predictions.
