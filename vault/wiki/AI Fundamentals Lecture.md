# AI Fundamentals Lecture

## Summary
This lecture provides a foundational overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI), covering its history, key concepts, and evolution. It emphasizes the shift from rule-based systems to data-driven approaches, particularly with the rise of deep learning and large language models like ChatGPT. The lecture highlights the importance of data scale, model training, and the underlying mathematical principles – statistics, probability, linear algebra – that underpin AI development. It also explores different learning paradigms, including supervised and unsupervised learning, and touches upon data quality and self-sanitization.

## Key Concepts
*   **Pattern Recognition:** AI's ability to identify patterns in data, as exemplified by a chess grandmaster’s intuition.
*   **Data-Driven Learning:** The core of modern AI, relying on training data to generate predictions and generalize to new situations.
*   **Scaling Laws:** The observation that increasing data volume, compute power, and model size consistently improve AI performance.
*   **Large Language Models (LLMs):** AI models like ChatGPT, based on deep learning and transformer architectures, excelling at natural language processing.
*   **Supervised Learning:** A learning paradigm where the model is trained on labeled data (input-output pairs).
*   **Unsupervised Learning:** A learning paradigm where the model is trained on unlabeled data and must discover patterns independently.
