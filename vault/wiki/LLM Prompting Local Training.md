# LLM Prompting Local Training

**Summary:**
This meeting discusses the assignment of training a small, local LLM model. Students are tasked with creating a "corpus" of text to train the model, evaluating its understanding through "evals" (small quizzes), and adjusting the corpus based on the model’s performance. The key takeaway is that while the model learns through exposure to text, simply feeding it the evaluation questions is considered cheating.

**Key Concepts:**

*   **Local LLM:** Training a language model on a personal computer rather than relying on cloud-based services like Claude or GPT.
*   **Corpus:** The collection of text data used to train the LLM. The larger and more relevant the corpus, the better the model’s understanding.
*   **Evals (Evaluations):** Small tests or quizzes designed to assess the model's understanding of specific concepts. These are used to refine the corpus and training process.
*   **Temperature:** (Mentioned in the title but not detailed in the transcript) - A parameter controlling the randomness of the model's output, which was not discussed in this meeting.
*   **Context Windows:** (Mentioned in the title but not detailed in the transcript) - The amount of text the model can consider when generating a response, which was not discussed in this meeting.
*   **Vocabulary:** The set of words the model understands and can use. Expanding the vocabulary through the corpus is crucial for improving performance.
