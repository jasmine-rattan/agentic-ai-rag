# LLM Training Fundamentals

## Summary

This meeting covered the foundational concepts of training large language models (LLMs), focusing on embeddings, synthetic data generation, and model inspection. Participants are tasked with training their own LLM, starting with 3,000 training steps and exploring techniques like increasing training steps and utilizing synthetic data created by tools like Babel, Astra, or SOLR. The goal is to gain a practical understanding of the "black box" nature of LLMs and to develop skills in iteratively improving model performance through experimentation and data manipulation.

## Key Concepts

*   **Embeddings:** Representing text in a way that captures semantic meaning, allowing the LLM to understand relationships between words and concepts.  The meeting illustrates how embeddings align concepts like “recommend” with “review” based on textual input.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Utilizing generated text data (created through tools like Babel, Astra, or SOLR) to supplement and augment the training data, potentially improving model performance.
*   **Model Inspection:**  The process of evaluating and understanding changes in the LLM’s behavior after training, including testing its responses and analyzing its outputs.
*   **Training Steps:** The number of iterations the LLM undergoes during the training process, with the meeting highlighting the use of 3,000 steps as a starting point.
